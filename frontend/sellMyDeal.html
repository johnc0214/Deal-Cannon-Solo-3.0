Buyer Outreach Tip

What Should You Send a Cash Buyer?

  • Property address or general location
  • Asking price
  • Estimated repair amount
  • After-repair value
  • Property photos
  • Access instructions
  • Earnest money requirements
  • Closing timeline
  • Proof-of-funds requirements
  • Your contact information
Before You Market Your Deal

Before advertising a property or contractual interest, confirm that your agreement gives you the right to market or assign the contract. Clearly disclose that you are selling or assigning a contractual interest when required.

Follow all applicable real estate licensing, advertising, privacy, email, telephone, text-messaging, and Do Not Call laws. Rules differ by state and municipality. Consider having a qualified real estate attorney review your contracts and marketing process.

Contact data should only be used for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with the applicable platform's terms and privacy requirements.
